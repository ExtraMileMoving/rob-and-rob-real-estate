Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

This policy explains what information I collect through this website, how I use it, and the choices you have. "I," "me," and "my" refer to Robert McClelland, a REALTOR® licensed in Michigan and affiliated with eXp Realty.

Information I collect

I only collect information you choose to give me through the contact form, which may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Whether you're interested in buying or selling, your timeline, and a property address
  • Anything you write in the message box
  • Whether you agreed to receive calls and text messages

Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, for security and performance.

How I use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the real estate services you ask about
  • To contact you by phone, email or text message, according to the choices you make on the form
  • To keep records of client communications as required by my brokerage and by law

Text messages and calls

If you check the consent box, I may call or text you at the number you provide about your real estate inquiry. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also opt out by contacting me directly.

I do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

When information is shared

I do not sell your personal information. I share it only when needed to serve you or when required:

  • With my brokerage, eXp Realty, as part of required recordkeeping and supervision
  • With service providers who help run this website and process form submissions, only to perform those services
  • With others involved in your transaction, such as a lender, title company or moving company, only with your knowledge and as needed to complete it
  • When required by law, regulation or legal process

How long I keep it

I keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, and meet brokerage and legal recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask me to update or delete your contact information, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing or calling me using the details below. Records I'm legally required to keep may be retained.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
