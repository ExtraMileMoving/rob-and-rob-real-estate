Part of every listing

Your listing and your move, planned together

Most sellers end up juggling an agent, a moving company and a closing date that keeps shifting. When you list with me, one person keeps all of it lined up.

I coordinate the movers and schedule them around your closing. There's no payment for the move until closing, and a closing credit is part of how we structure it. Buying instead? The same offer applies, timed to your purchase closing.

Talk about selling

Available to clients under a signed listing or buyer agreement. Credit is applied at closing and is subject to written terms and conditions provided at signing. Not valid with all moving providers.

Who does the actual moving?

A local, professional moving company I'm comfortable recommending. I coordinate the scheduling so the move lines up with your closing.

When do I pay for the move?

No payment until closing. Payment for the move is deferred and handled at closing on your sale or purchase.

How does the closing credit work?

We agree on the credit when we sign your listing or buyer agreement, and it's applied at closing. I'll walk you through the exact numbers so there's nothing unclear.

Does it work if I'm buying?

Yes, the timing is just different. When you're buying, the move is scheduled around your purchase closing and settled there. If you're selling one home and buying another, I coordinate the move around both closings.

Mortgage experience

I used to decide whether to approve or deny mortgages

Before becoming an agent, I was a mortgage underwriter: the person at the lender who reviews a buyer's finances and decides whether their loan gets approved. That background helps my clients in a few ways.

Pricing with the appraisal in mind

Most buyers need a loan, and lenders generally won't lend more than a home appraises for. I keep that in mind when we talk about price, so there are fewer surprises later.

Offers that are likely to close

The highest offer isn't always the best one. I know what a solid loan approval looks like, so I can help you judge which offers are most likely to make it to closing.

Straight answers

You'll get the numbers and my honest read on them. If I think a price or a plan is a mistake, I'll tell you and show you why.

About me

Hi, I'm Rob.

I'm a REALTOR® with eXp Realty, based in Farmington Hills and serving buyers and sellers across Livingston, Washtenaw, Wayne, Oakland and Macomb counties. Before real estate I was a mortgage underwriter, deciding whether to approve or deny people's mortgage applications.

That work taught me how lenders and appraisers look at a home's value, how financing really works, and where deals tend to fall apart. Now I use it to help buyers and sellers make decisions with clear numbers behind them.

I'm a lifelong Michigan resident, born and raised in Ann Arbor. From flipping houses in high school to contracting in college to mortgage lending, I've seen many different sides of the real estate world, and I bring all of it to every client I work with.
